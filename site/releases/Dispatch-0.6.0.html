Dispatch 0.6.0

  • Comments make sense on their own. A comment notification opens on the post it's about, with a short preview and the whole post a click away, then the comment it replies to, then the comment itself.
  • Views on every post. Reader rows show how many people have read a post, including P2 and saved posts.
  • Longer previews. Post rows show three lines of the post, and their details are easier to read.
  • Yellow stars. Likes are WordPress.com's yellow now.
  • Saved no longer shows a bookmark on every row, since everything there is saved.
