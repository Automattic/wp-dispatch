Dispatch 0.3.0

  • New tab names. Blogs is now Following, and P2s is now Automattic.
  • Switch tabs with the arrow keys. In the Reader, ← and → move between Following, Automattic and Saved.
  • Back to the top. Press ⌘↑, or click the ↑ button that appears beside the tabs once you've scrolled.
  • Calmer comments. The comments drawer stays put when you move between posts instead of sliding in again each time.
