Dispatch 0.2.0

  • Reply from the app. Open the comments drawer to comment on a post, or press Reply under any comment. Send with ⌘↩.
  • Like comments. Every comment shows a heart and its like count.
  • Like posts. Use the heart in the reader toolbar, or press l. These are real WordPress.com likes, and liking an x-post likes the original post.
  • Starred sites are gone. They only existed inside Dispatch and were easy to confuse with likes. Saved posts are still here.
  • Comments are easier to read, with more space between lines.
