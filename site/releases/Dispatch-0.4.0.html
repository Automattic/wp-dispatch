Dispatch 0.4.0

  • Hovercards. Rest on an avatar or a site icon to see who it is. Site cards have a Read button that shows just that site's posts.
  • Likes are stars. Just like WordPress.com, with a little celebration when you like something.
  • Comment counts in the Reader, next to the likes.
  • X-posts show up once. A post cross-posted to several P2s you follow no longer repeats.
  • Images on private P2s now load in the reader.
  • Tidier comments. Short comments get short bubbles, and Like and Reply line up with the text.
  • Restart in the ⋯ menu, and the resize grip stays under the list when the reader is open.
